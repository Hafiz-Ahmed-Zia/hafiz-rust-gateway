# Code of Conduct

We want Hafiz Rust Gateway to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source community.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect different backgrounds and experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized or abusive conduct;
- protect private security reports, personal information, and confidential user data;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when evaluating vendors, benchmarks, or commercial proposals.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Repeated or severe misconduct can result in a temporary or permanent ban.

An independent/private conduct contact must be established before broad public community launch. Until that contact exists, the repository should not advertise a mature enforcement process it cannot provide.
