# Research Index

Research exists to challenge the product idea, not to decorate a predetermined feature list.

## Sequence

1. Read `01-enterprise-ai-gateway-research-plan.md`.
2. Give `02-pro-model-master-research-prompt.md` unchanged to the external Pro research model.
3. Save the complete response and source list under `external/` without rewriting it.
4. Compare it with `03-independent-baseline-research.md` using `04-pro-research-comparison-template.md`.
5. Update `05-claim-ledger.md`; downgrade claims that lack primary evidence.
6. Run interviews from `06-design-partner-interview-guide.md`.
7. Re-score the opportunity and decide whether to proceed, narrow, pivot, contribute to an incumbent, or stop.

## Evidence rule

Vendor documentation can establish what a product claims to support, but it cannot establish customer demand or comparative superiority. Performance claims require reproducible tests. Market claims require direct customer evidence or a transparent primary study.
